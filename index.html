Workflow

From Idea to Upload
in Four Steps

01

Describe Your Vision

Tell ContentOS what you want in plain language. "A 12-minute animated story about a robot learning to paint, Pixar style, with three main characters." That's it.

02

AI Builds the Shot Graph

Our Director AI breaks your story into scenes, plans camera angles, selects locations from your Universe, and creates a complete production timeline — like a real film pre-production.

03

GPU Cluster Renders

4 NVIDIA L40S GPUs render keyframes, composite characters into scenes, and generate video segments in parallel. Multi-model pipeline: FLUX for images, Qwen for editing, LTX for video.

04

Review, Approve, Publish

Watch your video, tweak if needed, and hit publish. ContentOS handles SEO, thumbnail, description, tags, and scheduling. Your content goes live while you plan the next one.
